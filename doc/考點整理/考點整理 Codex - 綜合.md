### `00 Developement and Anatomy`

1. **Glomerular development與 barrier anatomy**：capillary-loop development、podocyte survival factors、slit-diaphragm components/topology、GBM layers、GEnC markers、podocyte–endothelial crosstalk。[`SKH`, `MMH`]
2. **Life-course nephron biology**：low nephron endowment complications、renal aging mechanisms與 histologic change。[`DALIN`]
3. **Parathyroid embryologic descent**：inferior gland隨 thymus下移、ectopic adenoma定位與 mediastinal TB mimic。[`KCGMH`]
4. **TSC developmental／genetic multisystem disease**：renal CT burden、pregnancy/estrogen association與 surgery-indication signal；source本身未列 criteria。[`KMUH-C`]

### `00 Table of contents and General contents`

### `01 Na, K, & Acid-Base`

1. **Water balance／hyponatremia**：osmotic/nonosmotic regulation、osmoreceptor dysfunction、fluid-restriction failure predictors、exercise-associated symptomatic hyponatremia、pregnancy Na change、TIH。[`TCVGH`, `TPE-TZUCHI`, `TPEVGH`, `TMU`]
2. **Polyuria algorithms**：water vs osmotic diuresis、DI vs primary polydipsia；童綜合 MD新增的 `>1000 mOsm/day`／`PNa ≤140` 不是 PDF原 branch。[`TUNG`]
3. **K homeostasis／aldosterone**：hyperkalemia抑制 ammoniagenesis、dietary Na/K adaptation、aldosterone paradox、pseudohypoaldosteronism、renin/aldosterone profiles、SGK1–ENaC、succinylcholine、pseudohyperkalemia。[`TCVGH`, `TUNG`, `NTUH`, `KMUH-C`]
4. **Metabolic alkalosis**：generation/maintenance、urine chloride algorithm、vomiting/diuretics/decreased DRA、Gitelman/Bartter、pregnancy／laxative/base mixed cases。[`YD`, `TMU`, `NCKU`]
5. **RTA與 urine ammonium surrogates**：Sjögren distal RTA、UAG/UOG、TDF Fanconi/type 2 RTA、ifosfamide pattern、topical carbonic-anhydrase inhibitor mixed physiology、urinary diversion/ileocystoplasty。[`SKH`, `NCKU`, `TPE-TZUCHI`, `TMU`, `SHH`]
6. **Diuretics**：loop/thiazide mechanisms、albumin／tubular delivery、NSAID interaction、sequential blockade、diuretic resistance/adverse effects。[`TPEVGH`, `DALIN`]
7. **Toxic/metabolic acidosis**：MALA、DKA/HHS、toxic alcohol comparison signal、urine alkalinization需要 normokalemia。[`TPE-TZUCHI`, `NCKU`, `SHH`, `YD`, `TUNG`]
